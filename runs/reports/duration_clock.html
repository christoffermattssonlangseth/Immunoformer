Headline. EAE tissue carries two separable molecular clocks. Severity is acute and reversible — it resets at each relapse. Duration is cumulative and irreversible. A multivariate elastic-net clock predicts time-since-induction at Spearman +0.80 (leave-one-animal-out) even after severity is removed (permutation p = 0.020), and it strengthens — not weakens — when sex and cord region are additionally controlled (+0.86). The signature reads as the biology of an aging lesion field: neuron/myelin loss, a basement-membrane→fibrotic-scar switch, lymphoid organization, and a lipid synthesis→scavenging switch.

What this is — in plain language

The problem. Each mouse is sampled once, at one point in its disease, and given a clinical score for how sick it is at that moment. That score sees current disease activity but is largely blind to the damage piled up from earlier attacks. Two mice can look equally sick yet be at very different points in their disease history.

The idea. day_of_sacrifice (11–49 days post-induction) is a real measure of how long disease has run. Within the relapsing–remitting cohort the relapse cycle decouples time from severity (peaks recur at similar severity but later days), so we can ask: is there a molecular signal that tracks duration independent of severity? We train a clock to predict day-of-sacrifice from tissue, with severity statistically removed, and validate it by leave-one-animal-out cross-validation plus a shuffling test.

The answer. Yes — strongly, and it is not an artifact of severity, sex, region or batch.

The clock predicts duration beyond severity

Each dot is one held-out animal: its predicted duration-residual vs the truth. The points hug the diagonal across all three cord regions.

Clock B (severity-orthogonalized): LOAO Spearman +0.80, R² +0.67. Genes and day are residualized on the clinical score within each training fold, so the clock cannot cheat through severity. For contrast, predicting day from severity alone gives -0.52 — negative: later timepoints are not sicker, the relapse cycle resets acute severity. That decoupling is what makes duration identifiable. A cross-target control (predict score from day-residualized features) gives +0.84, confirming the transcriptome carries both axes separably.

Validation — the clock beats a shuffled null

Could a clock this good arise by chance from 33 animals? We shuffle the day labels and refit, 50 times. None of the shuffles come close.

Permutation null mean ρ = -0.07; observed +0.80; p = 0.0196 (floor for 50 permutations). The clock is not an over-fit. Day-of-sacrifice is also clean of technical batch within RR: Kruskal p(day) = 0.1379 (run_date), 0.7168 (animal multiplex), 0.2145 (region).

The clock is not a sex, region or severity artifact

Two nuisance axes could masquerade as time: sex (the signature includes the X-marker Xist) and cord region. Removing them makes the clock stronger, not weaker.

Fully-controlled clock (severity + sex + region, Xist dropped): Spearman +0.86, R² +0.73 — above the original +0.80. Sex is orthogonal to day (Xist-raw vs day ρ = -0.05; Mann-Whitney day~sex p = 0.1768), and region tracks severity more than time (see below). Removing these nuisance directions sharpens the temporal signal.

Region — a spatial gradient that aligns with severity

EAE has a spatial cord gradient. It is real, but it indexes how severe the tissue is, not how long disease has run — so it cannot be what the clock reads.

Cervical carries the most severe disease here (mean score C = 1.77 > T = 1.31 > L = 1.00); lumbar is sampled earliest. Region orders with severity (ordinal L<T<C vs score ρ = +0.28) but is not significantly tied to day (Kruskal p = 0.2145; ordinal vs day ρ = +0.18, p = 0.329). Because clock B already removes severity, the region gradient cannot drive it — confirmed by the fully-controlled clock above.

The biology — an aging lesion field

The multivariate clock picks non-redundant genes, so it reads as a coherent six-part chronic-progression program (↑ accrues with duration, ↓ declines; severity removed).

Where the clock lives — spatial localization

The clock says tissue tracks duration; this asks which cell types and niches carry it. We rebuild the same severity-orthogonalized clock independently inside each compartment (too few animals for a black-box attention net) and rank by held-out accuracy.

Duration is a tissue-wide chronic program that peaks in glia. Every compartment carries it (LOAO ρ +0.69 to +0.89), led by astrocyte (ρ +0.89, perm p=0.0196), oligo/myelin and myeloid — neurons and lymphocytes carry the least. Crucially, a clock built on cell-type proportions alone is far weaker (composition ρ +0.52, perm p=0.0392): the signal is cells changing state, not just shifting in number. The strongest spatial niche is niche3 (vascular/endo) (ρ +0.84, perm p=0.0196).

Each gene module sits in its expected cell type

The clock-gene modules are not diffuse — they map onto the cell types that produce them, which is what makes the tissue-wide signal mechanistically interpretable.

Lymphoid genes (Cxcl13, Mzb1, Jchain) in B/plasma; scar/matricellular (Thbs2, Ptx3, Serpine2) in astrocytes; basement-membrane (Col4a1/2, Eln, Cxcl12) in vascular/endothelial; myelin (Mog, Mal) in oligodendrocytes; foamy-lipid uptake (Lpl) in myeloid; cholesterol synthesis (Hmgcr, Idi1, Msmo1) in OPC/oligo; neuronal (Uchl1, Gad1, Slc17a6) in neurons.

Honest limits

n = 33, animal-level pseudobulk (spatial information collapsed) — LOAO + permutation null guard against over-fit but the cohort is small. RR only: the chronic arm is excluded because there day-of-sacrifice aliases run_date (batch). Sex cohort is imbalanced (7 vs 26) though orthogonal to day. The clock is a supervised regression onto a de-confounded temporal label, not a within-animal time course — there are no per-animal sequences in terminal cross-sectional data.

Next

The localization shows the clock is glia-led and state-driven, but the compartment clocks still aggregate each cell type to one number per animal. The finer follow-ups: (1) a within-lesion version using the radial core→rim→margin zones (does duration concentrate in the scar core?); (2) validation in the chronic arm with matched-batch sampling to break the day~run_date confound; (3) a publication-grade null (N_PERM≈1000) on the headline clock.
