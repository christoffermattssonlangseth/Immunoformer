Headline. Along the relapse cycle, genes split into two temporal classes: acute / oscillating (spike at every peak, fall back at remission, floor does not rise — resets each cycle) and ratchet / cumulative (remission floor rises cycle over cycle — survives clinical recovery). Hal is a strong acute oscillator: it spikes at PK1/PK2/PK3 and fully resets, so it tracks acute relapse activity, not cumulative damage.

Caveat — severity confound.
